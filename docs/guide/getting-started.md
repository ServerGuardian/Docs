# Getting started

::: warning Not open yet
Accounts for server owners open at launch. This is the planned path.
:::

Setting up takes four steps, in this order. **Players are only checked once every step is done.**

1. **Create an account and turn on two-step sign-in.** You need an authenticator app. Keys cannot be created without it.
2. **Accept the Data Processing Agreement.** ServerGuardian processes player data on your behalf, so this is required.
3. **Add a server.** Choose its game (BeamMP, FiveM or Minecraft), and whether it is youth-oriented. Choose what happens if ServerGuardian cannot be reached: **Let players in** (the default, safer for your community) or **Keep players out** (stricter).
4. **Create an API key** for the server. Most plugins need all three permissions the key offers. The key is shown once; keep it like a password.

Then [install the plugin](/install/beammp) for your game and set your [rules](/guide/rules). New accounts start with a 14-day trial of the Pro plan.

## Appeals

Players can appeal a block or a ban you recorded. You have **14 days** to answer; after that the ServerGuardian team takes over. You will see appeals about your own servers under **Appeals** in the portal. You never see who the player is.
