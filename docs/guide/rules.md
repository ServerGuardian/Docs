# Rules

Each server has its own join rules, set in the portal under the server's **Rules** tab. They decide who is turned away. You only ever learn pass or block, never why. Changes are limited to 10 an hour.

| Rule                                          | What it does                                                                                                                                                                          | Default                 |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| **Players banned on your servers**            | Turns away anyone with an active ban from any of your servers.                                                                                                                        | On                      |
| **Players banned by other server owners**     | Turns away players when **at least this many other owners** have an active ban on them. The lowest you can set is two: one owner's ban alone is not enough, in case it was a mistake. | On, at two              |
| **Accounts that look like a banned player's** | Turns away accounts that share an IP address or computer with a banned player. You choose **whose bans count** (only yours, or any server owner) and **how sure we need to be**.      | On, any owner, Balanced |
| **Standing needed to join**                   | A broad summary of a player's track record: Everyone, Building or higher, Established or higher, or Trusted only. Most servers let everyone in.                                       | Everyone                |
| **If a player says no to the hardware check** | On FiveM the hardware check is optional for players. You choose to let them in or turn them away.                                                                                     | Let them in             |

## How sure we need to be

The strength of an account match is a choice of three words, never a number: **Very sure** blocks least and is the most careful, **Balanced** is the default, and **Less sure** blocks more and will sometimes get it wrong. A shared network on its own is weak evidence and rarely enough to block anyone; a shared computer, or several signals together, counts for much more.

## When a rule gets it wrong

Shared computers and networks can cause mistakes. A blocked player can see that they were blocked and contest it. If staff find the link was wrong, that player is cleared of it for two years.
