# For players

If you play on a server that uses ServerGuardian, you can see what is held about your game accounts, and contest a block.

::: warning Not open yet
The player portal opens at launch.
:::

## Signing in

You sign in with the game account you play on. You enter your password on that platform's own site, so we never see it, and we do not ask for an email address. Steam sign-in comes first, and BeamMP sign-in is planned. FiveM players can use a Steam account that has been seen with their FiveM licence. Minecraft sign-in is not available yet. If you cannot sign in with any of these, contact us and we will help.

## What you can see

- **Your standing:** a broad summary (New, Building, Established or Trusted) and the signals it is based on: how long we have seen you, how many servers you have played on, and recorded actions. It is not a score and not a judgement of you.
- **Bans and warnings:** actions that servers recorded about you, with the reason the server gave.
- **Blocks:** when you were turned away, and why, in plain words.
- **Privacy and data:** download everything we hold about you as a file, delete it (needs a sign-in within the last ten minutes), and switch the optional hardware check on or off.

## If you were blocked or banned unfairly

Use **Appeals**. The server owner has 14 days to answer; after that the ServerGuardian team decides. If a block came from a link to another account, for example because you share a computer, say so: if staff find the link was wrong, you are cleared of it for two years.

Shared computers, schools and family PCs can look like ban evasion, and that is why appeals exist.

## What we do not collect

Chat. We do not receive or store what you say in games. A server owner is only ever told pass or block, never who you are or why.
