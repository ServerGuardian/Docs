# For hosting companies

If you rent game servers, you can offer ServerGuardian to your customers as an add-on. You set the customer up through our API, we email them an invitation, and **you** pay us, not them. Your customer gets the Pro plan while you cover them, or the Business plan if your agreement includes it.

## How it works

1. **We set you up.** We agree a price per covered customer per month (and a separate price for Business, if you will offer it), your payment terms, and a daily ceiling on new customers. You get a partner API key, shown once.
2. **You create a customer** when they buy the add-on from you: their email address, their server community's name, and optionally their servers. Use your own reference for the customer so the call is safe to repeat.
3. **We email the customer an invitation.** They choose a password, which also confirms their address. The link works for 14 days, and you can ask us to send it again.
4. **Cover starts when they accept.** From that moment they have Pro and you are billed for them, by the day. If an invitation is never accepted, you are never billed for it.
5. **You can change a customer's plan** between Pro and Business at any time, if your agreement has a Business price. It takes effect at once, for an upgrade or a downgrade.
6. **You end cover** when they cancel with you. They keep their account and its data, move to the Free plan, and are emailed. They can subscribe themselves if they want to keep Pro.
7. **We invoice you monthly**, for the days each customer was covered, at the plan they were on. You can read invoices through the API, and pay through Stripe or the way we agree.

## What you can and cannot see

You can see your own customers by your reference, whether each has accepted their invitation, and your usage and invoices. You **cannot** see a customer's players, bans, rules or anything inside their account, and you do not get their password or their API keys. They run their own account.

## What we ask of you

- Send us an email address only for a customer who has agreed that you pass it to us for this purpose. We use it to send the invitation and for account security, and we tell the customer who set the account up.
- Keep your partner key secret, like a password. Anyone with it can create customers you will be billed for. Tell us at once if it leaks and we revoke it.
- End cover when a customer stops paying you.
