    details: Your server is never told who a player is, who banned them, or why. That protects players, and it protects you.
  - title: Works where you already run
    details: Plugins for BeamMP, FiveM and Minecraft (Paper). If ServerGuardian cannot be reached, players are let in by default.
  - title: Fair to players
    details: Players can see what is held about their game accounts, download it, delete it, and appeal a block, with a person deciding.
